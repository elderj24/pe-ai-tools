How to read this, and its limits

  • Market ranges are placeholders built for this demo. Production would load a licensed private-equity compensation survey and size each company by revenue or EBITDA.
  • Equity = grant as a share of fully diluted equity × the company's current equity value from the fund's Q3 marks, spread over a five-year hold. It ignores any preferred return or hurdle, so it overstates what an option-like grant is worth today.
  • Rolled equity (what an executive reinvested at close) is not pay and should be excluded; the tool flags grants large enough to suggest it is mixed in.
  • Access: individual pay is visible to the talent team and deal leads only; anyone else sees function-level medians.
  • Open searches: several states, including Illinois, require pay ranges in job postings; confirm requirements for each company's state with counsel.
