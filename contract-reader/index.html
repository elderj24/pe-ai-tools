Diligence Contract Reader

Shows which terms in a contract put a deal at risk, and marks the clause behind each finding.

One reader, two rulebooks. For a commercial contract, Claude pulls the terms a buyer cares about and marks each clause. For a regulated or claims-heavy document (advertising and product claims, device or pharma promotion, an informed consent form), it checks every claim against the source documents and lists the required elements that are missing.
