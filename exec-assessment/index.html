Mission

Take Ivy from founder-led to a professionally run, multi-state platform with measurable clinical quality, ready for a sale in 2029.

Outcomes in three years

  • EBITDA from $7M to $13M, with at least half from existing sites
  • 12 new sites, each at break-even within 12 months
  • Measured clinical outcomes on every patient, reported to the board quarterly
  • A CFO and a clinical leader hired and retained
  • Founder moved to a board seat with a clean handover in year one

Panel ratings, 1 to 4 against the anchors

Each rater scores alone before the debrief. Change any rating to see everything downstream update.

References, filed against the scorecard

Calls are summarized by AI and checked against the call notes by the search partner. Each item says whether it confirms, complicates or contradicts the panel's view.

Weighted panel score

Dot = average of the three raters' weighted scores. Bar = lowest to highest rater, so a long bar means the panel disagrees.

Does the answer depend on the weights?

500 alternative weightings, each weight moved up or down by as much as half. Share of weightings in which each candidate comes out on top.

Where each candidate is strong and where the evidence is thin

Average panel rating. ⚠ = low confidence: raters two or more points apart, a contradicting reference, or no reference on a competency weighted 15 or more.
