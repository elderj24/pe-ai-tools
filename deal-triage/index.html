How the Beta would run

Intake

A shared deals mailbox, read in the firm's own enterprise AI account, not partners' personal inboxes. The firm's IT and compliance teams would review the setup before any real mail is read.

The deal screen

Each teaser or CIM gets the same deal screen: a mandate check, a first-pass view, and questions for the banker. The firm's own criteria replace the invented ones used here.

Firm memory

Returning companies match their last screen, and portfolio overlaps and add-on fits surface first. This needs the portfolio list and past screens from the CRM.

People decide

The Beta drafts. A person makes every call, and nothing goes to a banker or seller automatically.

Success test

Less time per teaser, and no pass that turns out to be a deal the partners wanted.
