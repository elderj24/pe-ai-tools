Thesis Sourcer

This tool scores a company list against a sector thesis and tells you what to do with each company: reach out now, build a relationship, watch or pass.

Set a sector thesis, name the platform and the current portfolio, and paste a company list from PitchBook, Grata or a conference roster. The page applies hard filters and scores size and ownership in code; Claude judges thesis and platform fit and checks the portfolio; the page then sorts each company into an action.

How the scoring works
Hard filters, in code

An exclusion term in the description, no revenue, or revenue above 1.5 times the ceiling drops the company, with the reason logged.

Size and ownership, in code

Size fit (out of 20) from revenue against the range: 20 inside it, at most 8 below the floor, sliding down above the ceiling. Ownership readiness (out of 15) from the ownership field: founder or family highest (15), privately held 12, sponsor-owned long enough to be in an exit window 10, venture-backed 9, corporate division 8, recently sponsor-backed 4, public company lowest (3).

Judgment, by Claude

Thesis fit (out of 40), platform or add-on fit (out of 25), role, the portfolio check, the next trigger and a first-call draft. Its read is labeled as a draft, never as fact.

Action, in code

70+ with a recent signal and no conflict: Reach out now. 70+ otherwise: Build relationship. 50–69: Watch. Under 50 or a pass: Pass. A portfolio conflict, or revenue below the floor, caps the action at Build relationship.
