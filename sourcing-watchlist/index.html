How it scores

Change the weights or the size ranges and every ranking updates.

Likely to trade: signal weights

Weights are rescaled to 100. Each signal scores 0 to 100 before weighting; see the right-hand panel.

How each signal scores

Hold vs norm
0 at three years under the sector's typical hold, 100 at three years over.
Fund age
0 at year 6 of the owning fund, 100 at year 12 or later.
Sponsor raising
100 if the sponsor is marketing a new fund, else 0.
Debt maturity
100 within 12 months, 60 within 24, 25 within 36.
Exit-prep signals
30 per signal (new CFO, banker, slower add-ons, CEO change, recap, sell-side QoE, continuation vehicle), capped at 100.

Fit

Fit = 40% sector focus (core subsectors score higher), 40% size in range for its role (falling off outside it), 20% platform link: 100 for a named add-on to a current platform, 80 for a new platform inside an active thesis, 30 otherwise. Defaults follow the firm's published $50M to $500M deal range.

Guardrails

A prompt for a call, not a prediction

Scores rank who to call first. They don't claim a sale is coming.

Signals are checked

Banker, CFO and fundraising signals come from licensed data and press, and a person confirms each one before it counts.

The relationship owner leads

No outreach goes out without the person who knows the CEO or sponsor; for an add-on, the platform CEO is part of the plan.

CRM writes are confirmed

Nothing reaches the CRM until a person confirms the list, owner and note. Existing records are matched first, so a send updates rather than duplicates.

Real data is licensed

In real use, ownership, fund and debt data come from the firm's existing sources (PitchBook, Grata, PE Info or similar) and the CRM.
